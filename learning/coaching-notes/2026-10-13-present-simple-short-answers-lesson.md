# Present simple short answers — material, example, guided practice

Material: use Yes/No + pronoun + do/does or don't/doesn't. Match the subject and auxiliary. Does she ...? Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't. Do they ...? Yes, they do. / No, they don't. When someone asks Do you ...?, reply I: Yes, I do. / No, I don't. Full answers are also grammatical; this lesson practises short answers without repeating the main verb.

Worked example: Does your brother cook dinner? Brother = he; does matches he. Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. Choose yes/no according to the facts; do not invent personal facts.

Guided personal practice: Do you use your phone for banking? Coach hint: you refers to the learner; respond I. Use one short answer that is true for the learner. Question uses learner's already volunteered topic, not a request for financial details. This is supported practice, not ability measurement.

After guided response, prepare one independent short-answer practice set. Mark invented scenarios as coach-created. Keep separate from original IELTS attempts and responses.
