## Material

For ordinary verbs in the present simple:
- I/you/we/they and plural nouns: do not (don't) + base verb.
- He/she/it and third-person singular nouns: does not (doesn't) + base verb.
- The main verb takes no -s after doesn't: She doesn't buy, not she doesn't buys.
- This lesson concerns ordinary verbs; be uses am/is/are not and is a separate pattern.

## Worked example

Positive: She buys local products.
Step 1: She takes doesn't.
Step 2: Put the main verb in its base form: buys becomes buy.
Negative: She doesn't buy local products.
Does carries the subject agreement, so the main verb needs no -s.
Compare plural: They don't buy local products.
