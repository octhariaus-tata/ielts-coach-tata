# Present simple questions — teaching before practice

13 October 2026, Asia/Jakarta. Coach-created lesson, not official IELTS. Rule version adaptation-1.2. Sources: preferences 2026-10-13-learner-english-teaching-sequence and 2026-10-13-learner-many-grammar-exercises; negative practice evidence/practice/2026-10-13-present-simple-negatives-set01.json.

Material: Do + I/you/we/they/plural noun + base verb? Does + he/she/it/singular noun + base verb? The ordinary main verb loses -s after Does. Be questions are a different pattern, not this lesson.

Worked example: She buys local products. Subject she → Does. Move Does before she; buys becomes buy. Does she buy local products? Compare They buy local products → Do they buy local products? Useful check: Does already carries agreement, so no -s on the main verb.

Guided: My sister works in a hospital. Ask a question. Hint: my sister = she; choose Do/Does then work/works. Write the whole question. Answer withheld until response. Supported learning, not an independent assessment.
