## Material

Use present simple for facts and habits. With he/she/it or a singular noun, ordinary verbs take -s/-es. With I/you/we/they or plural nouns, use the base form. This lesson uses help and buy; be and some other forms have their own rules.

- A tourist buys local products.
- Tourists buy local products.
- Tourism helps the economy.

The -s in tourists makes the noun plural. The -s in buys/helps matches a third-person singular subject. These are different jobs for -s.

## Worked example

A tourist ___ (buy) souvenirs.

Step 1: find the subject — a tourist.
Step 2: one tourist, a third-person singular noun.
Step 3: buy becomes buys.
Answer: A tourist buys souvenirs.

Compare: Tourists buy souvenirs. The plural subject takes buy.
